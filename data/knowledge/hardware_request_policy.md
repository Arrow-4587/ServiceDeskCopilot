# Laptop Hardware Procurement Policy

## Refresh Cycle
- Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
- Standard specs: Dell Latitude / Lenovo ThinkPad, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD.

## Peripheral Requests
- Monitors, keyboards, and mice can be requested via Service Desk portal.
- Manager approval is required for non-standard hardware requests exceeding $250.

# Microsoft Teams & MFA Troubleshooting

## Teams Audio/Video Issues
1. Open Teams Settings (`...` -> Settings -> Devices).
2. Verify correct Microphone and Speaker devices are selected.
3. Make a test call to verify audio levels.

## MFA Reset Protocol
If you lose your mobile phone or authenticator app:
1. Contact Service Desk Analyst to request temporary TAP (Temporary Access Pass).
2. Register new device at `https://aka.ms/mfasetup`.

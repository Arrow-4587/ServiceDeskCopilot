# Phishing & Security Incident Reporting Protocol

## Reporting Suspicious Emails
1. Click the `Report Phishing` button in Outlook ribbon.
2. If button is unavailable, forward email as attachment to `phishing@company.com`.

## Security Outage & Malware Response
- If ransomware or malware is suspected, disconnect ethernet cable and turn off Wi-Fi immediately. Contact SecOps hotline: 1-800-SEC-HELP.

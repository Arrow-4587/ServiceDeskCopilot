# Corporate Password Reset Policy

## Password Guidelines
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Must contain uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special symbols.
- Passwords expire every 90 days.

## Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR)
1. Navigate to `https://passwordreset.company.com`.
2. Enter your email and verify your identity using Microsoft Authenticator.
3. Choose a new password that has not been used in the last 10 rotations.
4. Account lockouts reset automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity.
